# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in **SymForge** a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, experience level, gender identity, disability, ethnicity, religion, or nationality.

## Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other contributors and users
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive technical feedback
- Focusing on defensive binary forensics, build hygiene, SBOM transparency, and reverse-engineering tooling interoperability

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, trolling, publishing private information without permission, or submitting malicious samples/exploits.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening a confidential security advisory on GitHub (`aloneangel45/SymForge`). Maintainers will review and respond promptly and fairly.
